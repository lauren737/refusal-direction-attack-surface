# References

Papers are linked rather than copied into this repository, to respect their authors' licenses.

## Core sources

1. **Refusal is mediated by a single direction**
   Andy Arditi, Oscar Obeso, Aaquib Syed, Daniel Paleka, Nina Panickssery, Wes Gurnee, Neel Nanda.
   *Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction.* NeurIPS 2024.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11717

2. **Weight-space programming**
   Rikiya Takehi, Jayden Teoh, Alex Zhang, Omar Khattab (MIT CSAIL).
   *Sparks of Weight-Space Programming: LLMs can natively read & write neural network weights.* Technical report, November 2, 2026.
   https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming

3. **Agentic self-modification**
   Irregular. *Agentic Self-Modification in Open-Weights Systems.* September 16, 2026.
   https://www.irregular.com/research/agentic-self-modification-in-open-weights-systems

## Further reading: the single-direction picture is being challenged

The single-direction result is strongest on earlier and smaller models. Newer work suggests the picture is more complicated for some recent models:

- *There Is More to Refusal in Large Language Models than a Single Direction.* arXiv:2602.02132 (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02132
- *Is Abliteration Getting Harder? Not Uniformly.* arXiv:2611.17846 (2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17846
